by LC

Chapter 1 - Making Plans

Jack and Jill were excited to go to the dog park again, hoping to see their newfound four-legged friend Spot.
As they crossed the street toward the entrance, they broke from a stroll into a jog,
having seen Spot's humans Dick and Jane in those signature neon windbreakers.
Spot's gaze met theirs, and the little terrier mix bounded over,
much to Jane's panic (she was always the cautious one in the couple).
To everyone's relief, Spot pulled short of the fenceline, yipping affectionately.

Jill had come prepared with treats, and as she squatted down to give head pats,
the scent of salmon drove Spot into a leaping frenzy,
his slightly muddy paws leaving streaks on the white jeans that Jack had bought for her birthday.

"Nothing a bit of detergent won't fix," Jack assured himself. It had been a difficult move to an unfamiliar city,
and the social contact was worth the laundry.

After Jane managed to wrangle Spot back onto leash,
the foursome found their way to a picnic table and made small talk under the afternoon sun;
for his part, Spot slobbered busily over his chew toy.
From behind his aviators, Dick floated the idea of visiting the vineyard in the next county over when June rolled around,
and though Jack was, if anything, a beer guy,
he nodded and crinkled his eyes just enough to look enthusiastic about the idea.
He glanced at Jill, whose seemingly-agreeing smile– after two years together, he could tell– belied a similar reluctance.

But they didn't know anyone else in town, and they didn't have anything lined up for the summer, so:
Siri, add Thousand Oaks Tour to the Google Calendar.
