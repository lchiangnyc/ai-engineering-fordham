# Distinguished Panel: A Quantum Leap in Cyber Threats: Are We Ready for What’s Next?

![Geoff Brown](/media/home/schools/gabelli-school-of-business/ICCS_2025_Geoff_Brown_headshot.jpg)


**Moderator: Geoff Brown**, CEO, Silobreaker

Quantum computing is on the horizon, promising immense computational breakthroughs but simultaneously introducing unprecedented cybersecurity risks. This session will delve into the immediate and future threats from quantum-capable adversaries, including the looming breakdown of classical cryptographic systems. Through insights from leading researchers and security practitioners, we'll analyze quantum-readiness benchmarks, explore practical post-quantum cryptography (PQC) migration strategies, and review government timelines like NIST's PQC standardization. The panel also will focus on vital methods for mitigating quantum risk to critical infrastructure and high-value data assets. Join us for this crucial discussion, tailored for both technical and strategic audiences, as we confront the urgent challenge: Can we secure our digital future before quantum threats become reality?
