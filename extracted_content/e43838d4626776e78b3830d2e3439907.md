# Libraries, Small-scale data curation and the Digital Humanities: lessons learned from Fordham/IHR Wohl Libraries collaboration

Drawing on experiences of preparing digital course material for DH library training as part of a collaborative Fordham/IHR project, Shaw and Strnadova will examine the specific challenges of curating data on a small scale, the practicalities of engaging in DH in a library setting, and how it relates to staff development. The session will demonstrate one of the outcomes of the project, a study of historical theatres on the Layers of London Platform.
